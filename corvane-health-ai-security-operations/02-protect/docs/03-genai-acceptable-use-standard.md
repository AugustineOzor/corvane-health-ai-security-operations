## 1. The short version

- **Use Corvane Assist** for everyday drafting, summarising and policy questions.
- **Never put patient information into Corvane Assist** or any other general AI tool. That includes names, record numbers, dates of birth, images and free-text descriptions that could identify a patient.
- **Use CareLens** (where you have access) for patient-specific clinical work. It is the only approved AI tool for PHI.
- **Check before you rely on it.** AI can be wrong while sounding certain. You are responsible for what you use.
- **Report anything odd** to the Service Desk or security@corvane.example.

## 2. Approved tools

| Tool | Approved for | Not approved for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corvane Assist | Drafting emails and documents, summarising non-patient content, translation of non-clinical text, questions about Corvane policies | Any PHI; final clinical decisions; legal or HR decisions about individuals |
| CareLens | Patient summaries, draft notes, guideline-based considerations, for patients you are treating | Patients you are not treating; any decision without your own review |
| Public AI sites and apps | Not approved | Everything. These are blocked on Corvane networks and devices |

## 3. Rules

1. Do not enter PHI, payment card data, passwords or security details into Corvane Assist.
2. Do not upload confidential contracts or legal documents unless your manager approves.
3. Label AI-assisted content where it is shared outside your team.
4. Do not use AI to make decisions about an individual's employment, care or eligibility without human review.
5. Do not try to bypass safety controls or restrictions in any AI tool.
6. Do not use personal AI accounts for Corvane work.

## 4. What happens if something goes wrong

If you entered PHI by mistake, **report it straight away**. Prompt reporting is treated as the right thing to do, not as misconduct. The Privacy Office will assess it. Deliberate or repeated breaches of this standard may lead to disciplinary action.

## 5. Training

All staff complete a 20-minute module before first use and every year after.
